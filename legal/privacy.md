# Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

This policy covers the Codex Live Share plugin and the hosted Live Share service (together, the "Service"). The Service is operated by Jhen-Ke Lin in Taiwan ("we", "us"). Contact: [contact email].

## What we do not collect

- **Shared content.** Files, edits, plans, transcripts and audio go directly between participants over encrypted peer-to-peer connections. They never reach, and are never stored on, our servers. When a relay is needed, it carries only encrypted traffic it cannot read.
- **Direct mode.** Direct mode uses no server of ours. Connection setup runs on the host's computer through a Cloudflare tunnel, and Cloudflare's own policies apply to it.
- **Transcription.** Your browser sends your microphone audio to OpenAI or Google using your own API key. Their policies govern that processing, and we never receive the audio, the text, or your key.

## What we collect for hosted mode

- **Account:** your GitHub account ID, username, display name and avatar URL, received when you sign in.
- **Credentials:** a one-way hash of your Live Share sign-in token. We do not keep your GitHub token.
- **Sessions:** room codes, and the display names, colors and random peer IDs that participants use to connect. These are kept only until a room has been empty for 24 hours.
- **Usage:** your plan, how many hosted rooms you have open, and monthly relay time, used to apply plan limits.
- **Network data:** IP addresses and request metadata, processed by our hosting provider for security and rate limiting.
- **Payments:** handled by our payment processor. We receive subscription status and billing details such as country and plan, never full card numbers.

## How we use it

We use this data only to run the Service: signing you in, connecting participants, applying plan limits, billing, preventing abuse, and answering your requests. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

## Service providers

We rely on:
- Cloudflare: hosting, relay and tunnels.
- GitHub: sign-in.
- Our payment processor: billing.

Each processes data under its own terms. Data may be processed outside your country.

## Data on your computer

The plugin stores settings, API keys, session state and transcripts on your computer, in `~/.codex-live-share`. Shared files stay in the folders you choose. You control and can delete all of it.

## Retention and your choices

- **Retention.** We keep account data until you ask us to delete your account. Session data is deleted automatically, as described above.
- **Your requests.** Email us to access or delete your data. We may keep records we must keep by law, such as billing records.

## Children

The Service is not intended for anyone under 16.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows when it last changed. Continuing to use the Service after a change means you accept it.
